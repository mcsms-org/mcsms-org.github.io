Join the Society

Build simulations, explore computational science, and collaborate on technical projects with a team of motivated students.

Note

No prior experience required. Curiosity and a willingness to learn are enough. We welcome students from all disciplines — physics, computing, mathematics, engineering, and beyond.

Flexible Commitment

Built for Busy Students

We understand that O/L, A/L, tuition classes, and schoolwork come first. MCSMS is designed with flexible participation, small tasks, and exam-season adjustments. You can still contribute even if you cannot attend every meeting.

Family Support

For Parents

MCSMS is not just a coding club. Students use mathematics, physics, ICT, and scientific thinking to build small computer models of real-world systems. The society supports academic development, problem-solving, teamwork, communication, and university readiness.
