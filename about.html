About MCSMS

Our Story

01

Background

The Simulation & Modelling Society (MCSMS) was established in 2026 at Maliyadeva College to provide students with opportunities to explore computational science through simulations, modelling, and collaborative projects.

What began as a small group of students sharing an interest in scientific computing has grown into a student-led community committed to learning by building, experimenting, and sharing knowledge.

02

Affiliations

MCSMS is based at Maliyadeva College and works in collaboration with the Maliyadeva College ICT Society to promote computational science, programming, and technology education across the student body.

Hands-On Learning

We believe the best way to understand a system is to build one. Every project starts from first principles — no black boxes.

Open Collaboration

Projects are owned by the whole team. Ideas, code, and credit are shared openly regardless of prior experience level.
