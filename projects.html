What We're Building

01

Conway's Game of Life

Exploring cellular automata and emergent complexity. We are building a zero-player grid simulation to observe how incredibly complex, unpredictable patterns can arise from just a few simple, localized rules.

02

Particle Collisions

A 2D physics sandbox to model kinematics, momentum, and energy transfer. By accurately simulating elastic and inelastic collisions, we're laying the groundwork for understanding thermodynamics and gas dynamics.

03

Boids (Flocking Behavior)

Simulating artificial life and swarm intelligence. By programming just three basic steering behaviors — separation, alignment, and cohesion — we can recreate the fluid, synchronized flocking seen in birds and fish.
