Boids Simulation

Boids is an artificial life program, developed by Craig Reynolds in 1987, which simulates the flocking behaviour of birds. The complexity of Boids arises from the interaction of individual agents (the boids) adhering to a set of simple rules: Separation, Alignment, and Cohesion.
